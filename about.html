Owner & electrical contractor

Thomas Wynn

Thomas Wynn is the owner of Wynn’s Electrical Service and an experienced electrical contractor with more than 20 years of experience in healthcare electrical systems and facility improvement projects.

His professional background includes electrical installations and repairs, LED lighting upgrades, electrical panel schedule updates, troubleshooting, and specialized projects in hospital environments.

Thomas brings a hands-on approach to project execution, with an emphasis on safety, precision, problem-solving, and collaboration. His experience working with healthcare facilities and other project stakeholders reflects his commitment to delivering dependable electrical solutions that meet each project’s requirements.
